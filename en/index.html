Pedigree software for racing pigeon breeders

Pigeclub is pigeon pedigree software for breeders who want an orderly loft without retyping paper pedigrees. Import a pedigree from a photo or a PDF, check the recognised data and save the pigeon's ancestry to your records.

From there you print pedigrees when you sell a bird, show it or plan pairings. Ring numbers, colours, sex and ancestors are all in one place.

Who it is for

Racing pigeon breeders with a dozen or a few hundred birds, breeders who send youngsters to one loft races, and anyone moving from a notebook or an old program to modern pedigree software.
