Pigeon pedigree template: what it must include

Published 5 November 2026

In short: A racing pigeon pedigree template: which details to include, how to lay out the generations and how to prepare a pedigree for printing.

There is no single official pigeon pedigree form, but breeders have used the same layout for decades. Stick to it and buyers will read your pedigree at a glance.

Layout

The pigeon on the left. To the right, the sire on top and the dam below, then four grandparents and eight great-grandparents.

The pigeon's details

  • ring number with country code, region and year,
  • sex and colour,
  • name, if the pigeon has one,
  • breeder's name, club and contact details.

Ancestors' details

For each ancestor, the ring number and the most important results: race, distance, position. Leave empty fields empty; do not guess.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong digits in a ring number.
  • Missing year in the ring number.
  • Sire and dam swapped: the sire always goes on top.
  • Results without the size of the race: 1st of 50 is not 1st of 5,000.

Stop retyping pedigrees. Pigeclub imports them from a photo or a PDF. See Pigeclub.

Questions and answers

What should a pigeon pedigree include?

Ring number, sex and colour, breeder details, and the sire, dam, grandparents and great-grandparents with their ring numbers and key race results.
