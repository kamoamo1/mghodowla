Pigeon pairing: how to plan with the pedigree

Published 5 November 2026

In short: Racing pigeon pairing: how to pair birds using pedigrees and results, avoid close inbreeding and strengthen your lines.

Good pairing starts with the pedigree, not with how the bird looks.

Check relatedness

Compare the cock's and the hen's pedigrees. Shared ancestors in the second or third generation can be a deliberate breeding decision. Shared parents are usually a mistake.

Pair results, not just blood

Look at the ancestors' race results. A pair from two lines that fly similar distances gives more predictable youngsters.

Record every pairing

Write down pairs, dates and youngsters. The youngster's pedigree then builds itself from the parents' data.

Stop retyping pedigrees. Pigeclub imports them from a photo or a PDF. See Pigeclub.

Questions and answers

How do I avoid inbreeding when pairing pigeons?

Compare both birds' pedigrees and avoid pairs with shared parents. Pedigree software shows common ancestors at a glance.
