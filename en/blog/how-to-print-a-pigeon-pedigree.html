How to print a pigeon pedigree

Published 5 November 2026

In short: How to print a pigeon pedigree for a sale or a show: what a clear pedigree contains and how to prepare it in pedigree software.

A buyer wants to see where a pigeon comes from. A clear, printed pedigree builds trust and adds value to the bird.

What a pedigree should contain

Ring number, colour and sex, breeder details, and the sire, dam, grandparents and great-grandparents with their ring numbers and best results.

Printing from software

In Pigeclub the ancestry is already recorded, so you print a pedigree in a moment, with no retyping and no risk of a wrong ring number.

Paper and format

A4 is usually enough. For a sale, use heavier paper: the pedigree will sit in the buyer's folder for years.

Stop retyping pedigrees. Pigeclub imports them from a photo or a PDF. See Pigeclub.

Questions and answers

What paper should I print a pigeon pedigree on?

A4 is standard. Use heavier paper for pedigrees that go to buyers.
