Pigeclub features

Everything a racing pigeon breeder needs, in one program

Pigeclub runs your whole loft: records, pedigrees, pairings and race results. The key difference from other programs: you do not retype pedigrees, you import them.

Pigeon records

Every bird with ring number, year, sex, colour and status: racing, breeding, sold. Search by ring number and see the pedigree and results at once.

Pedigree from a photo

Photograph a paper pedigree with your phone. The software recognises ring numbers, ancestors and notes; you just check the result. How to take a good photo.

PDF import

Pedigrees from sellers often arrive as PDF. Import the file directly.

Pedigree printing

Print a clear pedigree with ancestors and results for a sale or a show. What a printout should contain.

Pairing

Plan pairs, see shared ancestors of cock and hen, and record the youngsters from each pairing. How to plan pairings.

Race results

Record races, distances and positions. The best results go into the pedigree.

One loft races (OLR)

Keep the pedigree of every bird you send to a one loft race and add its results. How to prepare.
